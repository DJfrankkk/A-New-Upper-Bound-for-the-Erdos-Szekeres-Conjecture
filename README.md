## Abstract

Let $ES(n)$ denote the smallest integer such that every set of $ES(n)$ points in the plane in general position contains $n$ points in convex position. In 1935, Erdős and Szekeres conjectured that $ES(n) = 2^{n-2} + 1$, and in 1960, they established the corresponding lower bound. A breakthrough on the upper bound was achieved by Suk in 2017, who proved that

$$
ES(n) \leqslant 2^{n+O(n^{2/3}\log n)}.
$$

In this article, we further improve the upper bound by showing that

$$
ES(n) \leqslant 2^{n+O(n^{1/3})}.
$$

This repository contains the [Lean 4 formalization](ESVerify/) accompanying the [paper](A%20New%20Upper%20Bound%20for%20the%20Erd%C5%91s%E2%80%93Szekeres%20Conjecture.pdf). The authors of this paper are Yifan Hu, Guokuan Shao, and Guozhen Shen.
